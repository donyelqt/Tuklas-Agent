fix: a sub-question's supporters must come from ITS OWN hits

The SPEC-CONFORMANCE review lens found that select_evidence was fed the hits
of ALL sub-questions pooled into one list, and asked about all of them
together.

A hit's lex score is relative to the query that retrieved it. So a node
retrieved for sub-question A carries lex > 0 relative to A - and the
supporter rule (retrieval.md sec 3: score >= 0.15 AND (lex > 0 OR
identifier-hit)) accepts lex > 0 for ANY sub-question. Pooling therefore let
one sub-question's nodes satisfy another sub-question's support check: the
gate could pass for a question whose own hits supported nothing.

Measured on two sub-questions with DISJOINT top-10 hits ("What is
succession?" and "What is the registration of chattel mortgages?"): 3 of the
second sub-question's 8 supporters came from the FIRST sub-question's hits.
After the fix: 8 of 8 supporters of each sub-question come from its own hits.

The hits are now recorded per sub-question, and select_evidence is called
per sub-question with that sub-question's own hits; the verdicts are merged.
The retrieval contract is untouched - select_evidence still evaluates
whatever hits it is given, and the agent now asks the right question of it.

The merged kept set is also cut to the top-8 evidence window
(retrieval.md sec 5): the agent assembles ONE context, and without the cut
the merged set would carry 16 nodes - double the window - inflating the
prompt. The gate's supporters are never dropped by the cut; only
non-supporting nodes are.
